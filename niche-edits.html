Niche Edits FAQ

A niche edit (also called a curated link or link insertion) is a backlink placed inside an existing article that is already published and ranking on a relevant website. Instead of writing new content, we insert your contextual backlink into live pages that Google already trusts and indexes. Guest posts build new authority. Niche edits borrow existing authority. Both have a place in a serious SEO strategy.
Niche edit services typically range from $60 to $125 per placement depending on the domain authority (DR 10-40+) and organic traffic of the publishing site, with white-label bundles available from $50/link for agencies.
Niche edits are safe when placed on real, editorially-managed sites with genuine traffic and natural link profiles — which is exactly what our vetting process ensures. Google penalizes manipulative link schemes and PBN networks, not legitimate editorial placements on quality sites.
We pitch site owners and editors with a value exchange: either a fee for the editorial update, a reciprocal content arrangement, or a resource addition that genuinely improves their existing content for readers. This editorial approach ensures the link is placed naturally within the flow of the article.
A niche edit places your link in an article that's already published and ranking, while a guest post creates a brand-new article from scratch. Niche edits are faster because the page is already indexed and has existing authority — guest posts give you more control over the surrounding content.
Most clients see initial movement within 2-4 weeks of link placement, with full impact typically visible by week 6-8 as Google recrawls the page and processes the new link signals. Because the page is already indexed, results come faster than guest posts.
Pre-placement approval is available as an optional upgrade for an additional fee. Our standard process handles site selection and placement based on your niche and target page — we vet every site through our 4-layer quality check so you can trust the placement meets your standards. If you need to review and approve each placement before it goes live, we can accommodate that with custom pricing.
We guarantee all niche edit placements for 12 months. If a link is removed within that period, we replace it at no cost with a comparable or better placement. Your investment stays protected.
