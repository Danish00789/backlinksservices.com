### Rhino Rank
No dedicated white-label page. Their "Built for Agencies and Freelancers" messaging is sprinkled across service pages (+40% cheaper, 150% ROI for resold links) but not centralized.

**Key gap:** Only LinkPanda has a proper white-label conversion page. Building a better one is a competitive advantage.

## White-Label Page — Structure Blueprint

### Section 1: Hero
**Purpose:** Instant recognition — "this page is for ME" (agency owner/SEO consultant)
**H1:** White Label Link Building for SEO Agencies
**Subhead:** "High-authority backlinks delivered under your brand. You sell the SEO. We build the links. Your clients never know we exist."
**Trust bar under subhead:** "Trusted by X+ agencies · Links from $50 · White-label reports included"
**CTA:** "Get Agency Pricing" + "Book a Partnership Call"

**Why this works:** The "your clients never know we exist" line is the core promise. Every agency owner's fear is their white-label partner getting discovered and poaching clients.

### Section 2: The Agency Problem
**Purpose:** Agitate the specific pain of running an SEO agency
**Content:** 3 short pain points (2-3 sentences each):

1. **"You're turning down link building work."** — Clients ask for it. You know they need it. But you don't have the team, the publisher relationships, or the time to deliver quality links at scale.
2. **"You tried outsourcing and got burned."** — Spammy links. Disappearing placements. Ghosting vendors. Reports your clients would laugh at. The industry is a minefield.
3. **"Your margins are getting squeezed."** — Hiring in-house link builders costs $50K+/year each. Freelancers are unreliable. You need a partner, not an employee.

**Bridge line:** "That's why agencies partner with us."

### Section 3: How the Partnership Works
**Purpose:** Simple, no-surprises process. Demystify the relationship.
**4 steps with agency-specific framing:**

1. **You onboard your client** — You set the strategy, pricing, and deliverables. We stay invisible.
2. **We build the links** — Guest posts, niche edits, brand mentions. All manual outreach. All vetted.
3. **You get white-label reports** — Clean, branded PDFs with live URLs, domain metrics, and placement details. Your logo, your colors, your voice.
4. **Your client sees results** — Rankings improve. Traffic grows. You look like the hero. We stay in the background.

### Section 4: What You Get (Agency Benefits)
**Purpose:** Specific deliverables, not vague promises
**Format:** 6 benefit cards with icons

1. **White-Label Everything** — Reports, dashboards, communication. Your branding end to end.
2. **Agency-Only Pricing** — From $50/link in bundles. Built-in margin so you profit on every placement.
3. **No Minimum Orders** — Buy 1 link or 100. Scale up and down with client demand.
4. **You Approve Every Site** — See the domain before we place. Veto anything that doesn't meet your standard.
5. **Guaranteed Placements** — Link goes down? We replace it. Free. Your client never knows there was an issue.
6. **Dedicated Account Manager** — One point of contact who learns your clients, your niches, your standards.

### Section 5: Services You Can Resell
**Purpose:** Show the full menu. Agency owners think in terms of "what can I package and sell?"
**Format:** 3 cards mirroring the main service pages but framed for resellers

| Service | What You Sell It As | Your Cost (from) | Suggested Resell |
|---------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Guest Posts | "Authority Content Placements" | $50/link | $100-150/link |
| Niche Edits | "Curated Link Insertions" | $XX/link | $100-150/link |
| Brand Mentions | "Brand Authority Building" | $XX/mention | $100-200/mention |

**Margin math callout:**
"At $50/link cost and $150/link resell — a 10-link monthly package nets you $1,000/month in pure margin. With zero fulfillment work."

### Section 6: Agency Pricing
**Purpose:** Transparent pricing tiers for agencies. No "contact us" hiding.
**Format:** Simple tier table

| Bundle | Links/Month | Price/Link | Total/Month | Your Margin at $150 Resell |
|--------|-------------|------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Starter | 5 | $60 | $300 | $450 |
| Growth | 10 | $55 | $550 | $950 |
| Pro (Most Popular) | 20 | $50 | $1,000 | $2,000 |
| Scale | 50 | $45 | $2,250 | $5,250 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom |

**Pricing notes line:** "All prices include white-label reporting. No setup fees. No contracts. Cancel anytime."

### Section 7: Why Agencies Stay (Retention Proof)
**Purpose:** Address the unspoken fear — "you'll take my clients"
**Content:** 4 specific commitments:

1. **We never contact your clients.** Ever. All communication goes through you.
2. **No branding on reports.** Your logo. Your company name. Your email. We don't exist in the deliverables.
3. **Your client relationships are yours.** We don't pitch, upsell, or cross-sell to anyone you introduce us to.
4. **We sign NDAs.** Standard. No hesitation. Your client list is your business.

### Section 8: Case Study (Agency-Specific)
**Purpose:** Show an agency that scaled with white-label links
**Format:** Agency name (or anonymized), what they needed, what we delivered, results
**Example narrative:**
"An 8-person SEO agency was turning away 3-4 link building requests per month because they couldn't fulfill in-house. Within 60 days of partnering with us, they added link building as a $3,000/month retainer offering. Six months in: 12 active link building clients, $36K/month new revenue, zero additional hires."

### Section 9: FAQ (Agency-Specific Objections)
**8 questions:**
1. How do you make sure my clients never find out about you?
2. What does the white-label report look like?
3. Can I set my own pricing for my clients?
4. What if a client asks for something outside your standard services?
5. Do you require a contract or minimum commitment?
6. How do you handle rush orders?
7. What niches can you build links in?
8. What happens if a link gets removed — how do you handle it with my client?

### Section 10: Final CTA
**Purpose:** Convert the agency owner who's read everything
**H2:** "Ready to Add Link Building to Your Agency's Offering?"
**Subhead:** "White-label links from $50. No contracts. Your brand, our fulfillment."
**CTA:** "Get Started — Free Agency Account" + "Book a Partnership Call"
**Trust line:** "NDA available · No minimum orders · Cancel anytime"

You are an SEO copywriter specializing in B2B service pages. Write the complete page copy for a White Label Link Building landing page targeting SEO agency owners and freelance SEO consultants.

Domain: backlinksservices.com
Service: White-label link building — SEO agencies resell our guest posts, niche edits, and brand mentions under their own brand. We never contact their clients.
Target audience: SEO agency owners (5-50 person agencies), freelance SEO consultants, digital marketing agencies adding SEO. These are sophisticated buyers who've been burned by bad vendors before.
Pricing: From $50/link in bundles. Volume discounts at 20+ and 50+ links/month.
Brand voice: Direct, professional, agency-to-agency. No marketing fluff — these buyers smell it instantly. Trustworthy, transparent, "we've been on your side of the table."

## COMPETITIVE CONTEXT
Your page competes against LinkPanda's white-label page (linkpanda.com/white-label-link-building/) — the strongest in the market. They use: high-DR logos, live engagement portfolios, 12 partnership principles, founder story. Match their depth but be cleaner and more direct.

## PAGE STRUCTURE
Write copy for each section:

1. HERO: H1 + subhead + trust bar + CTA (60 words max)
   - H1 must include "white label link building"
   - The "your clients never know we exist" promise must appear

2. THE AGENCY PROBLEM: 3 pain points + bridge line (80 words)
   - Turning down work, burned by vendors, margin squeeze

3. HOW THE PARTNERSHIP WORKS: 4 steps with agency-specific framing (100 words)
   - You onboard → We build → You get reports → Client sees results

4. WHAT YOU GET: 6 benefit cards, 1-2 lines each (100 words)
   - White-label everything, agency pricing, no minimums, approval control, guarantees, dedicated manager

5. SERVICES YOU CAN RESELL: 3 cards with cost + suggested resell price (80 words)
   - Guest Posts, Niche Edits, Brand Mentions
   - Include margin math example

6. AGENCY PRICING: Simple tier table (see strategy document for structure)

7. WHY AGENCIES STAY: 4 specific commitments (80 words)
   - Never contact clients, no branding on reports, client relationships are yours, NDAs standard

8. CASE STUDY: Agency-specific narrative with real numbers (100 words)
   - Agency size, problem, solution, revenue impact, timeframe

9. FAQ: 8 questions with concise answers. Write for snippet extraction.

10. FINAL CTA: Strong headline + subhead + trust line + buttons

## CONSTRAINTS
- Total copy: 700-1,000 words
- Primary keyword "white label link building" in H1, first 100 words, and 3-4 times naturally
- Secondary keywords: "link building for agencies," "white label backlinks," "link building reseller" — each once, naturally
- Active voice. Short sentences. No jargon without explanation.
- No: "leverage," "synergize," "cutting-edge," "best-in-class," "industry-leading"
- Yes: specific numbers, process details, margin math, concrete commitments
- Every section must pass the "would an agency owner care about this?" test
- The "we never contact your clients" promise must be explicit and prominent — it's the #1 agency fear

## SEO SPECS (OUTPUT THESE FIRST)
- Meta title (exact): White Label Link Building for SEO Agencies | Reseller Links from $50
- Meta description (155-160 chars)
