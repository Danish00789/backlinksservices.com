Frequently Asked Questions About Guest Post Placements

A guest post is a new article written and published on a relevant website in your niche, with a natural, in-content link back to your site. Unlike PBNs or link farms, guest posts connect you with real websites that have editorial standards, organic traffic, and audiences in your industry. When done right, guest post backlinks build topical authority and send qualified referral traffic — which is why Google recommends them as a legitimate SEO method.
Guest post services typically range from $70 to $135 per placement depending on the domain authority (DR 10-40+) and organic traffic of the publishing site, with white-label bundles available from $50/link for agencies.
