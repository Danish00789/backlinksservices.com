# UPDATED PRICING STRATEGY

## No Separate Pricing Page

Pricing lives on each service page. This is actually stronger for SEO — each service page is a complete commercial destination with its own pricing, rather than forcing an extra click.

## Homepage Pricing Hints (Only)

The homepage shows starting prices to anchor value — not full pricing tables.

### Recommended Homepage Pricing Placement:

**In the Services section (3 cards):**
```
Guest Posts          Niche Edits          Brand Mentions
Real articles on     Links in existing    Your brand cited on
niche-relevant       ranking content.     real publications.
sites.               Faster results.      AI + Google authority.

From $70/link        From $XX/link        From $XX/mention
[Learn More →]       [Learn More →]       [Learn More →]
```

**In the White-Label section:**
```
White-Label Pricing
Same links, your brand. From $50/link in bundles of 10+.
[View Agency Pricing →]
```

**In the final CTA area:**
```
"Backlinks from $50/link (white-label). Guest posts from $70. 
No minimum orders. No contracts."
[Get Started →]
```

### Rules for Homepage Pricing:
- Show starting price only, never full tiers
- Each price is a link to the relevant service page
- White-label price always shown as a contrast to direct pricing
- Never list more than 3 price points on the homepage
- Price anchor serves as a trust signal ("they're not hiding pricing") — not a conversion tool
