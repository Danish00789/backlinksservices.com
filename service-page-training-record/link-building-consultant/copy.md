## How Link Building Consulting Works

**1. Strategy**
We review your site, your target pages, and your current link profile. We identify the DR range and niche categories that will move the needle, then agree on a placement plan before outreach begins.

**2. Outreach**
Our team contacts publishers in your niche directly. We do not use public PBNs or link farms. Every site that accepts a placement gets vetted for organic traffic and editorial standards.

**3. Placement**
A writer produces a 700+ word article built around your link. The placement goes live on the publisher's site as a genuine editorial piece. You approve the anchor and URL beforehand.

**4. Report**
You receive a placement report within two weeks of order confirmation. It includes the live URL, DR at time of placement, organic traffic figure, anchor used, and your destination URL. If a link is removed within the guarantee period, we replace it free.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does a link building consultant actually do?**
A link building consultant audits your existing backlink profile, identifies gaps and opportunities, and plans a placement strategy tied to specific target pages and keywords. The role is part analyst, part outreach manager. We handle all of it — strategy through placement through reporting.

**How much does link building consulting cost?**
Our placements run from $70 (DR 10–30) to $95 (DR 31–40) to $135 (DR 40+) per link. There is no setup fee and no monthly retainer. You pay per placement. Agencies ordering 10 or more links at a time qualify for white-label reporting at no added cost.

**How long does it take to get a link placed?**
The standard turnaround is two weeks from order confirmation. That covers outreach, article writing, editorial approval, and live placement. Complex niches or high-DR targets occasionally take a few days longer; we will tell you upfront if that applies to your order.

**What does "placement" mean — is it a sponsored post?**
A placement is a dofollow contextual link inside a 700+ word article published on a third-party site. Some publishers label contributing content; others do not. We focus on sites with genuine organic traffic and editorial standards rather than sites that exist only to sell links.

**Do you guarantee niche relevance?**
Yes. We match publishers to your site's vertical before pitching. If we cannot find a relevant publisher at the DR tier you ordered, we will tell you rather than force an off-topic placement.

**Is there a minimum order?**
No minimum for direct clients. You can order a single placement to evaluate quality. The only minimum is for white-label agency accounts, which require 10 or more links to access unbranded reporting.

**What happens if a link gets removed?**
We replace it free. If a publisher removes your link after placement, we will find an equivalent replacement on a comparable site at no additional charge. We track live placements and flag removals proactively when we spot them.

**Can I hire you as a link building consultant on an ongoing basis?**
Yes. Many clients order in batches monthly as part of a sustained link building strategy. We can advise on cadence, anchor distribution, and DR mix across campaigns. Contact us at hello@backlinksservices.com to discuss a recurring arrangement.
