## How It Works

**1. Strategy**
You tell us your target URL, preferred anchor text, and niche. If you are unsure what anchor mix to use, we can advise — but the decision is always yours.

**2. Outreach**
Our team contacts site owners and editors in relevant niches. We do not use PBNs or link networks. Every site we reach out to is independently owned and editorially reviewed before we pitch.

**3. Placement**
A writer produces a 700+ word article that fits the host site's editorial standards. Your link is placed contextually inside that article. We confirm live URL before reporting.

**4. Report**
You receive a placement report showing the live URL, domain metrics, anchor text used, and the URL your link points to. White-label clients receive a report formatted for client delivery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a local link building service?**
A local link building service acquires backlinks for businesses that want to rank in a specific city, region, or service area. The links come from sites that are topically or geographically relevant to your target market, which reinforces your local authority in Google's eyes.

**How much do your backlinks for local SEO cost?**
Placements are priced by domain rating: $70 for DR 10–30 sites, $95 for DR 31–40 sites, and $135 for DR 40+ sites. All tiers include the same core features — only the domain authority and traffic threshold change.

**What does "placement" actually mean?**
A placement is one live, dofollow backlink inside a 700+ word article published on a third-party site. The article is written for that site's audience. Your link appears in context — not in a sidebar, author bio, or footer.

**How long does it take?**
Standard turnaround is two weeks from the time your order is confirmed. Complex niches or high-DR targets occasionally take slightly longer. We will tell you upfront if that applies to your order.

**Do you guarantee the links will stay live?**
We offer a free replacement if a link is removed after placement. Links are placed on editorially managed sites, so removal is uncommon — but if it happens, we replace it at no charge.

**Can I use this service if I run an SEO agency?**
Yes. We offer white-label delivery for agencies ordering 10 or more links. Your client-facing reports carry no BacklinksServices.com branding. The 10-link minimum applies to white-label orders only — individual consultants and direct clients have no minimum.

**Will these links help with guest posting on local blogs specifically?**
Where relevant, we do place links through guest posting on local blogs and region-specific publications. However, we do not guarantee every link will land on a locally focused blog — niche relevance to your industry is the primary targeting criterion, with geographic relevance applied where possible.
