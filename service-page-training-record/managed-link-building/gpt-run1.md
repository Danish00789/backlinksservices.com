### What is included in your managed link building service?

We handle campaign strategy, publisher outreach, article creation or coordination, contextual link placement, and reporting. You provide the target URLs, anchors, and niche requirements; we manage the outreach process from start to finish.

### How much does the service cost?

Placements range from **$70–$135**. DR 10–30 placements cost $70, DR 31–40 placements cost $95, and DR 40+ placements cost $135.

Each tier includes a 700+ word article, anchor and URL control, a dofollow contextual link, a two-week turnaround, and free replacement if removed.

### What is the turnaround time?

The standard turnaround time is **2 weeks** after your order details are confirmed. Publisher schedules can occasionally affect delivery, and we will communicate with you if a placement requires additional time.

### What does “placement” mean?

One placement means one live, dofollow contextual backlink within a published article on a qualifying third-party website. It does not mean a footer link, sidebar link, author-bio link, or unlinked brand mention.

### How do you determine niche relevance?

We review the website’s primary topic, published content, and audience before pursuing a placement. Some placements may be directly within your niche, while others may be on closely related websites where your link fits the article naturally.

### Do you offer white-label link building?

Yes. Agencies can use our link building outreach service under their own brand with a minimum order of 10 links. Direct, non-agency clients have no minimum order.

### What should I expect from a backlink outreach company?

A backlink outreach company should explain what you are buying, provide verifiable placement details, and avoid promising specific rankings or traffic increases.
