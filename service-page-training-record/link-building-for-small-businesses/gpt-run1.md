A placement is one published article containing one contextual dofollow backlink to your chosen URL. You select the target page and anchor text, and we include the link naturally within the article.

The standard turnaround time is two weeks. This covers prospecting, publisher communication, content creation, and publication.

There is no minimum order for small businesses and other direct customers. You can begin with a single placement.

### How do you choose niche-relevant websites?

We review your industry, target page, services, and audience before selecting websites. A relevant placement should have a reasonable connection to the subject of your business or the content being promoted.

We do not treat Domain Rating alone as proof that a website is suitable.

Yes. We can shape a campaign around local service pages and location-focused search goals while seeking relevant contextual placements.

This service focuses on editorial backlinks. It is not a replacement for local citations, Google Business Profile optimization, or other parts of local SEO.

### Can you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. Backlinks are one part of search performance, alongside content quality, technical SEO, competition, and search intent.

We guarantee the agreed deliverables: qualifying placements, contextual dofollow links, controlled anchors and URLs, reporting, and free replacement if a delivered link is removed.
