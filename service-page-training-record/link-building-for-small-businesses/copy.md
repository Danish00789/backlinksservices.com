## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does "placement" actually mean?**
A placement is a published article on a third-party site that contains a dofollow link to your page. The article is written around a relevant topic, your link is included in the body copy, and the article stays live permanently. You receive the URL once it's published.

**How much do your link building services for small business cost?**
Placements start at $70 for DR 10–30 sites and go up to $135 for DR 40+ sites. There are no setup fees, no retainers, and no minimums for direct clients. You pay per placement.

**How long does it take to receive my links?**
Standard turnaround is 2 weeks from the time your order is confirmed. Most placements go live within that window. If there's a delay, we'll notify you before the deadline passes.

**What if a link gets removed?**
We offer free replacements on any link that is removed within a reasonable period after placement. If a site takes down the article or removes the link, we place a comparable one at no additional cost.

**Is this service white-label? Can I resell it?**
Yes. If you're an agency offering link building services for SMB clients, we can fulfill under your brand. Reports will be unbranded and formatted for you to deliver directly. The minimum for white-label orders is 10 links.

**How do you pick which sites to use?**
We vet sites for three things: domain rating, organic traffic from a third-party tool, and editorial standards. Sites need to have real content published by real contributors. We do not use sites we own, private blog networks, or sites that exist purely to sell links.

**Will one or two links actually move my rankings?**
It depends on your niche, your current authority, and who you're competing with. Link building is cumulative — a single strong placement rarely produces overnight results. What placements do is give Google a consistent signal that your site is worth ranking. Most small businesses see incremental gains over 60–90 days as links are indexed and weighted. We won't promise a specific ranking because no one can guarantee that honestly.
