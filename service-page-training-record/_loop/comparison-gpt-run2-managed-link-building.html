Frequently Asked Questions

**What does "managed link building service" actually include?**

It includes strategy (matching your URLs and anchors to the right link targets), manual outreach to real publishers, article coordination or writing, placement, and a live-link report. You don't touch any part of the outreach process — we handle it end to end.

**How much does it cost?**

Placements range from $70 (DR 10–30) to $95 (DR 31–40) to $135 (DR 40+). There are no setup fees or monthly retainer fees on direct orders. White-label agency plans start at 10 links/month.

**What is your turnaround time?**

Standard turnaround is 2 weeks per placement from the time we confirm your order details. Complex niches or high-DR targets can occasionally take longer, and we'll tell you in advance if that's the case.

**What does "placement" mean exactly?**

A placement is a single live dofollow link inside the body copy of a published article on a qualifying third-party site. It is not a mention without a link, not a nofollow link, and not a link in a footer or author bio.

**How do you ensure niche relevance?**

Before we pitch any site, we check its topical focus, recent content, and audience. We won't place a link to a B2B SaaS tool on a fishing blog because the DR is convenient. Fit is part of the vetting process, not an afterthought.

**Is this service white-label? Can I resell it?**

Yes. Agencies can order under their own brand with a minimum of 10 links per month. Reports are delivered without BacklinksServices.com branding so you can forward them directly to clients.

**What happens if a link goes down?**

We offer a free replacement if a placed link is removed. If we can verify the link was live and has since been taken down by the publisher, we replace it at no additional cost.

**Do you work in any niche?**

We work in most commercial niches. The main exceptions are content that is legally restricted in most markets or that violates standard publisher guidelines. If you're unsure whether your niche qualifies, email us before ordering.
