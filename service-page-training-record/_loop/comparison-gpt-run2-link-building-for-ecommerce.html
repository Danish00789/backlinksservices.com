A placement is a single live dofollow link inside a published article on a third-party website. The article is written around a topic relevant to the host site, and your link appears in the body copy — not in a sidebar, footer, or author bio. You receive the live URL once the article is published.

**How long does it take to get a link placed?**

Standard turnaround is two weeks from order confirmation. If anything causes a delay, we'll notify you directly. We don't pad timelines to look faster than we are.

**Do you build backlinks for Shopify stores specifically?**

Yes. Whether your store runs on Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, or a custom platform doesn't affect what we do — we're building links to your product pages, category pages, or homepage. The CMS doesn't matter. What matters is the target URL you give us.

**What is the white-label option and who is it for?**

The white-label option is for link building ecommerce agencies and SEO consultants who resell our services under their own brand. We deliver plain placement reports with no BacklinksServices.com branding. The minimum for white-label orders is ten links. Pricing is the same per-placement rate.

**How do I know if the links are actually helping my rankings?**

We don't promise ranking outcomes — no honest link builder does, because rankings depend on your entire SEO profile, your competitors, and Google's algorithm. What we promise is a real, live dofollow contextual link on a site with verified organic traffic. Tracking ranking changes over time is something you do in your own analytics or rank tracker.
