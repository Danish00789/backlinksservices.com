What a Backlink Audit Service Covers

Most sites accumulate links over time with no strategy behind them. Directories, low-quality syndications, irrelevant forums — they add up. Our backlink analysis services surface every issue and give you a ranked action plan.

**Toxic Link Detection**

We flag links from spammy, irrelevant, or penalized domains. You get a prioritised disavow list ready to upload to Google Search Console — no guesswork required.

**Niche Relevance Review**

A link from a pet blog to a SaaS product sends the wrong signal. We score your existing links by topical relevance so you know which placements are actually contributing to authority.

**Gap & Opportunity Analysis**

We compare your profile against top-ranking competitors to show you where you're underweight. You walk away knowing exactly which link types and DR ranges to prioritise.

**White-Label Ready Reporting**

Agencies get clean, unbranded PDF reports they can hand to clients as-is. Every finding is documented. Every recommendation is sourced. Nothing invented.

How Our Backlink Audit Service Works

**1. Profile Crawl & Data Pull**

We export your full backlink profile from industry-standard tools, cross-referenced across multiple data sources to minimise gaps.

**2. Link-by-Link Analysis**

Each linking domain is scored on authority, traffic, relevance, and spam indicators. We don't rely on a single metric — we look at the full picture.

**3. Toxic Link Flagging & Disavow File**

Any link that poses a risk gets flagged with an explanation. We prepare a ready-to-submit disavow file and document the reasoning behind every decision.

**4. Action Plan Delivery**

You receive a structured report: what to disavow, what to keep, what gaps to fill, and a prioritised link-building roadmap tied to your actual ranking targets.

Frequently Asked Questions

**What does a backlink audit service actually include?**

You get a full crawl of your backlink profile, toxic link flagging with a ready-to-submit disavow file, a niche relevance score for your existing links, a competitor gap analysis, and a prioritised action plan. Everything is documented with sources — no black-box outputs.

**How much does a backlink audit service cost?**

Audit pricing depends on the size of your profile and the depth of competitor analysis required. Link placements that follow the audit range from $70 per placement (DR 10–30) to $135 per placement (DR 40+). Contact us for an audit-specific quote.

**How long does the audit take?**

Most audits are delivered within 5–7 business days. Link placements recommended in the audit have a two-week turnaround from order confirmation.

**What does a "placement" mean?**

A placement is a single dofollow contextual link published inside a 700+ word article on a live, indexable website. The article is written around your target topic. You control the anchor text and the destination URL. The link lives in the editorial body — not a sidebar, not a footer.

**Do you guarantee the links stay live?**

Yes. If a placed link is removed within 12 months, we replace it at no charge with a comparable placement. We track every link we place and flag removals proactively.

**What is the minimum for white-label reporting?**

Agencies qualify for white-label reporting — unbranded PDF deliverables — at a minimum of 10 link placements per month. There is no white-label minimum for the audit report itself; that is always delivered unbranded.

**How is your backlink analysis service different from using a tool like Ahrefs myself?**

The tools give you data. The audit gives you interpretation. We cross-reference multiple data sources, apply manual relevance scoring, and produce a disavow file that accounts for context — not just raw metrics. A low-DR link from a topically relevant, real-traffic site is not the same as a low-DR link from a spam directory. We treat them differently.

**Can you audit a competitor's backlink profile?**

Yes. Competitor gap analysis is included as part of the standard audit. You can also order a standalone competitor profile review if you want to benchmark before building.

FAQ

What do your backlink audit services include?

We review your backlink profile to identify questionable links, unnatural patterns, irrelevant referring domains, anchor text concerns, and authority gaps. We then use those findings to recommend practical next steps and guide future placements.

Not every weak or spam-looking backlink requires action. We focus on links and patterns that merit closer review rather than labeling every low-metric domain as toxic.

How much does a backlink placement cost?

Pricing ranges from $70 to $135 per placement.

DR 10–30 placements cost $70, DR 31–40 placements cost $95, and DR 40+ placements cost $135. Every tier includes a 700+ word article, anchor and URL control, a dofollow contextual link, two-week turnaround, and free replacement if the link is removed.

What does “placement” mean?

A placement is one published article containing one contextual dofollow backlink to your chosen URL. The link appears naturally within the article rather than in a sidebar, footer, author biography, or standalone directory listing.

How long does the service take?

The standard turnaround time is two weeks. This covers outreach, content creation, publisher coordination, placement, and reporting.

A publisher may occasionally require revisions or take longer to publish. If that happens, we will communicate the status rather than claiming the placement is complete before it is live.

Do you guarantee that toxic links will be removed?

No. We can identify links that deserve review and recommend an appropriate response, but removal depends on the website owner.

A disavow file may be appropriate in limited situations, but it should not be used automatically. Google already ignores many low-quality links, so we avoid recommending unnecessary action without context.

How do you choose niche-relevant websites?

We consider the website’s subject, audience, published content, organic traffic, and relevance to your target page. DR is used as a pricing tier, but it is not the only factor considered.

A relevant website with genuine content is generally more useful than a higher-metric site with no connection to your niche.

Do you offer white-label backlink analysis services?

Yes. Agencies can order white-label backlink audit and placement services with a minimum order of 10 links. You receive clear reporting that can be shared with clients under your own process.

Direct clients do not have a minimum order.

Can I choose the anchor text and target URL?

Yes. You can provide your preferred anchor text and destination URL. We may recommend an adjustment if the anchor appears unnatural, does not fit the article, or could create an overly aggressive anchor profile.
