## Frequently Asked Questions

**What exactly is a "placement"?**
A placement is one dofollow link published inside the body of a 700+ word article on a third-party site. The article is written for that site, includes your link contextually — meaning it reads naturally within the content — and remains live permanently. You receive the URL when it goes live.

**How much does B2B link building cost?**
Our placements range from $70 to $135 each, depending on the domain rating and organic traffic tier of the host site. DR 10–30 is $70, DR 31–40 is $95, and DR 40+ is $135. There are no setup fees, no retainers, and no minimum order for direct clients.

**How long does a placement take?**
Standard turnaround is 2 weeks from confirmed order. We don't rush outreach — quality placements take time to negotiate and produce correctly.

**Do you do guest posts for B2B niches?**
Yes. Guest post B2B placements are a core part of what we do. The article is written for the host site's audience, which means it performs better editorially and is less likely to be removed over time. We cover SaaS, professional services, logistics, manufacturing, finance, and a range of other B2B verticals.

**What if a link gets removed?**
We replace it free of charge. If a host site takes down an article or changes a link to nofollow, we find an equivalent placement at no additional cost to you.

**Can I use this as a white-label link building service for my agency clients?**
Yes. We offer white-label fulfillment for B2B SEO agencies with a minimum of 10 links per order. You receive reports in a neutral format you can brand and deliver directly to clients. Email us to discuss white-label setup.

**How do you pick which sites to place links on?**
We filter for niche relevance first, then check domain rating and organic traffic against the tier you've ordered. We don't use private blog networks or link farms. Every host site is a real, editorially maintained publication. If a site doesn't meet the tier criteria, we don't use it.

**Do you write the articles?**
Yes. Each placement includes a 700+ word article written for the host site. You control the anchor text and destination URL. You don't need to supply content, though you can share any brand or topic guidelines you want us to follow.
